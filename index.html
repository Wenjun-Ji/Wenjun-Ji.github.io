About Me

Hi, I am Wenjun Ji. I am currently a Master's student at the School of Software & Microelectronics, Peking University, advised by Prof. Qingni Shen. I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Nankai University in 2026, where I worked as a research intern at Media Computing Lab (2024.05 – 2026.06), focusing on camouflaged object detection and AI security under the guidance of Prof. Deng-Ping Fan and Prof. Qing Guo.

My research interests include computer vision, AI security, and physical adversarial attacks. My research aims to build intelligent systems that are both theoretically grounded and practically effective, with the ultimate goal of advancing AI safety and reliability in real-world deployments.
